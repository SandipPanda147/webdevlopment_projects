BEYOND THE EVENT HORIZON

Enter the Black Hole.

A black hole is an object whose gravity is so intense that even light cannot escape once it crosses the event horizon.

The glowing structure around it is an accretion disk: extremely hot matter orbiting and falling toward the black hole.

Kalpana Chawla

Born in Karnal, India, Kalpana Chawla became the first woman of Indian origin to travel to space. She flew on STS-87 in 1997 and STS-107 in 2003.
